# Charity Born of Love

Charity born of guilt is not truly charity.
It seeks first to quiet the ache within oneself.

But charity born of Love is different.
It forgets itself and looks only to the need of another.
Its joy is not in easing a guilty conscience,
but in seeing another heart restored and healed.

The outward act may appear the same,
yet God sees the difference hidden within the heart.

Christ died so we will no longer live for ourselves.
Christ lives so we are able to live and love in freedom and abundance.

Jesus said,
"If you love Me, you will keep My commandments."
This is more than a command; it is a promise.

Once our hearts are filled with God's love that is done through the Holy Spirit.
Obedience blossoms naturally and bears fruit.

Love does what fear never can.
