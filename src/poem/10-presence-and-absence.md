# Presence and Absence
Wisdom is not knowing everything—<br>
but knowing the presence<br>
and the absence of the One.

Faith in God<br>
is not about understanding everything,<br>
but trusting the relationship<br>
with the One who loves us.

Love never discourages—<br>
but faith in others does.

In the absence of our control<br>
comes the presence<br>
of His freedom and peace.

Inspirations : <br>
