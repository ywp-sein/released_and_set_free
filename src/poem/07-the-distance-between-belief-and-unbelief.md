# The Distance between Belief and Unbelief 

The distance between <br>
belief and unbelief <br> 
is eternity.<br> 

Our lifetime <br> 
may be too brief to apprehend it;<br> 
Eternity <br> 
is too vast to comprehend.<br> 

But to reconcile that distance,<br> 
To turn a wandering heart,<br> 
Is to save a soul from eternal darkness. <br> 

The distance between <br> 
belief and unbelief <br> 
is faith. <br> 

The world around us <br> 
may be too overwhelming to endure;<br> 
The fear of the roaring lion <br> 
may be too strong to withstand; <br> 

But to reconcile that distance,<br> 
Requires but one humble cry:<br> 
"I believe; help my unbelief." <br> 

The distance between <br> 
belief and unbelief <br> 
is the Cross. <br> 

Our pride may be too loud to silence;<br> 
Our sins may cling too closely to cast aside; <br> 

But to reconcile that distance,<br> 
Is to deny ourselves, <br>
take up our cross, <br> 
And follow the One<br>
who died upon the Cross <br>
for us. <br> 

---

Scripture inspiration:<br> 
2 Corinthians 5:18-21, James 5:19-20,
Mark 9:14–29, 1 Peter 5:8–10, Matthew 16:24–27, Hebrews 12:1–2
