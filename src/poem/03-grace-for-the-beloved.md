# Grace for the Beloved

View our lives through the vast macro<br>
Of His eternal purpose sublime.<br>
Trusting Him fully in every micro<br>
Along the faithful path He has prepared.<br><br>

Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God,<br>
Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace—<br>
Holy Majesty beyond all names and all praise,<br>
Yet stooping low to call me beloved.
