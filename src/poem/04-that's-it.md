# That's It

After many trials,<br>
and through Word, visions, and dreams,<br>
I understand: I know more of how God plans.<br>
That’s it.<br>

Through suffering and pain,<br>
through tears and brokenness,<br>
I understand: I know more of how God feels.<br>
That’s it.<br>

Through every encounter,<br>
through all that touches my senses,<br>
I understand: I know more of the language God speaks.<br>
That’s it.<br>

After all of this,<br>
through all I have seen, heard and felt,<br>
I understand: I know God more.<br>
That’s it—<br>
and that is everything.<br>

---

*This simple yet deep satisfaction is my treasure. That's it.*
