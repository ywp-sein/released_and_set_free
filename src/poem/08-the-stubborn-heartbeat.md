# The Stubborn Heartbeat

We stand before a choice:

To see the world<br>
through an endless hunger<br>
for approval,

Or

To learn to see<br>
our Father's world,<br>
where He speaks to us<br>
everywhere.

The quieter<br>
our hearts become,

The clearer<br>
we hear His whisper of love.

When peace governs<br>
the stubborn heartbeat<br>
of our souls,

A glorious overture<br>
of freedom<br>
begins.

---

Note : Scripture inspired this poem, which is a reflection of Psalm 57:7, which reads:
"My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast; I will sing and make music."
