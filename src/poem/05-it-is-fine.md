# It's Fine

Where I am will not define me,<br>
What I’ve been through won’t confine me. <br>
Chains of injustice can’t fine me,<br>
Only the WAY I walk will refine me.<br>


“I do not know what I should do,<br>
but my eyes are on You.”<br>

I will not need to fight in this battle.<br>
It's fine.

---

*Note: A poem I wrote after I read II Chronicles 20:1-30*
