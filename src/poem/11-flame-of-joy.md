# Flame of Joy

Your consuming flame is my joy,<br>
A fire no veil can ever withstand.<br>
No good thing, nor grace nor glory, <br>
Will You withhold from those who walk upright.<br>

Your blazing fire is my hope,<br>
A flame no force can ever tame,<br>
Never quenched, never constrained,<br>
Yet forging my faith through the great “I AM.”<br>

Though tested by fire, my joy remains.<br>
The very flame of You<br>
Is my flame of joy.
