# The Unveiled Classroom 

A dream of exam papers torn apart,<br>
filled with accusations and hidden marks—<br>
traces of control written between the lines.

Justice stood at the table,<br>
exposing the hypocrisy of the cram school teacher,<br>
revealing what had long been disguised as order.

Wisdom returned sanity to the classroom,<br>
breaking the spell of blind obedience and fear,<br>
where silence had been crowned as discipline.

Hope prevailed over darkness,<br>
as the curtains were drawn back,<br>
and light at last shattered the shadows.

Freedom unlatched the windows and threw them wide;<br>
refreshing winds swept through the room,<br>
releasing the people from the suffocating pride.

Truth stands beyond the open windows,<br>
where fear no longer writes upon pages, <br>
and hearts once darkened and hopeless <br>
are made new in Light and Love.


---

Note:

A answered prayer of the verses of Psalm 18:28, which reads:
“For You will light my lamp;
the LORD my God will enlighten my darkness.”

I literally had a dream of a classroom where the bad exam papers were torn apart by a person standing on the table.
